The brief

Assemble '26 turns campus into a one-day arena. Six activities run in parallel across the lawns and the CS block — pick your track, team up, and see how far you get. No prior experience needed for any of them.

Built by first-years, for first-years. Every activity is judged by seniors and club mentors, and the top team in each track walks away with a prize and a spot in the club's core crew.

Pick your track

Every track is drop-in — register once, join whichever you like on the day.

TRACK 01

Cosplay contest

Build a look, bring the character to the stage. Judged on craft, accuracy, and stage presence.

TRACK 02

Trivia face-off

Teams of three race through lore, comics, and MCU rounds. Knockout format, fastest fingers win.

TRACK 03

Gaming arena

Console and PC brackets across two popular fighting and racing titles. Bring your own controller if you have one.

TRACK 04

Costume workshop

A hands-on session on prop-building and armor design, run by the design club ahead of the contest.

TRACK 05

Movie screening

Open-air screening at dusk with a snack stall and a costume-spotting game running alongside.

TRACK 06

Photo zone

Themed sets and props for the day. Free prints for the best three submissions, picked by public vote.
